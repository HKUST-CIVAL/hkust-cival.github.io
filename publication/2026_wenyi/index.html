Decoding Traditional Chinese Patterns with the WenYi Structured Corpus

ACM Journal on Computing and Cultural Heritage (JOCCH), 2026. Accepted.
Ziyao Gao, Huangjing Zhou, Wei Zeng
HKUST(GZ)
Abstract

Traditional Chinese patterns serve as a key visual language of Chinese culture, with unique aesthetic and symbolic value. However, the lack of comprehensive digital resources and structured representations has limited both large-scale cultural analysis and the culture-grounded reuse of patterns in contemporary design and generative workflows. In response, we construct WenYi, a structured corpus of traditional Chinese patterns. WenYi contains 2918 pattern instances covering 154 motifs across more than 2000 years of Chinese history, organized through a four-dimensional schema of Context, Content, Visual Form, and Cultural Semantics. The corpus is constructed through a hybrid annotation strategy that combines hierarchical metadata fields, controlled vocabularies, and open-ended semantic descriptions. Users can access the corpus through an explorer system we developed. We demonstrate the utility of WenYi through four cases: motif co-occurrence analysis, visual trend exploration, text-to-image model fine-tuning, and semantic-guided design ideation. The study provides a structured foundation for analyzing and reusing traditional Chinese patterns, while also discussing insights into structuring cultural complexity and making visual culture data explicit.
